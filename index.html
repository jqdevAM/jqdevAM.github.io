Pin16

Car diagnostics with an OBD-II adapter.

Plug an ELM327-class adapter into the port under the dashboard, and Pin16 reads what the car reports and explains it in plain words.

  • It works with no signal Every fault-code explanation ships inside the app. A car park under a building is where diagnostics usually happen.
  • It finds codes other apps miss Stored, pending and permanent codes, and the module's own fault memory — which is where a code like P1000 hides.
  • Your car's data stays on your phone No account, no server of ours. Reports leave only when you send one yourself.

Tintora

Identify any colour from a photo, offline.

Photograph a wall, a sofa or a tile and touch the colour you want. Tintora says what it is, in the codes designers and paint shops use, and what it will do in a room.

  • It measures what you touch A magnifier for the exact spot, an area rather than one noisy pixel, and a correction for the colour of the light.
  • It explains it in every system it knows HEX, RGB, CMYK, Lab and more, the nearest named colours, a Munsell notation and the nearest Federal Standard 595 paint.
  • Your photos stay on your device It never connects to the internet. Photos and colours are stored encrypted and leave only when you share them.
